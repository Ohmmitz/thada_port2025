PROJECT 

free-b

UX/UI • mobile app • Prototype • Design Systems • Mockup


A comprehensive elderly care service application that offers a full range of support — from professional caregivers to drop-off services, accommodation arrangements, and medical consultations — all conveniently integrated into one platform.

UX/UI process

Research

Identify target users by researching their pain points and goals.

wireframe

to focus on designing the website layout and arranging various components.

UI system

Develop a design system by establishing typography and color guidelines tailored to the application.

Prototype

Create both low- and high-fidelity prototypes, beginning with component-based design using Auto Layout.

Testing

Conduct prototype testing with team for review and revisions.

UX Thinking





problem

Designed a fully accessible application that empowers people with visual impairments to navigate travel and daily activities more independently and confidently.





solution

Implemented oversized buttons and cards with a simplified layout to support easier interaction, particularly for users with visual impairments. The white-and-blue color scheme enhances readability, while core features address essential needs such as caregiver, transportation, and travel services.





result

The app delivers a convenient user experience with valuable features, though additional refinement is required to fully resolve accessibility issues affecting visually impaired users.
