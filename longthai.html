pain point

  • Finding suitable and affordable accommodations across multiple websites requires a lot of time and effort.
  • Tourists cannot easily find complete information about attractions, restaurants, and rest stops in different areas.

Needs

  • Tourists want a platform that allows them to quickly and easily compare accommodation prices across multiple websites.
  • Tourists want complete and reliable information about attractions, restaurants, and rest stops.

experience

  • A platform with an easy-to-use and convenient design helps provide users with a pleasant and satisfying experience.
  • Users want complete and consistently updated information about accommodations, attractions, restaurants, and rest stops.

Insights

  • Searching for suitable and affordable accommodations across multiple websites requires significant time and effort.
  • Tourists cannot easily find complete information about attractions, restaurants, and rest stops in different areas.
