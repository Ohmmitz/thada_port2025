A financial services application with NFC-based Tab to Pay functionality, allowing users to deposit, transfer, and pay for products via their phones or NFC-enabled payment terminals. The app also provides tools for users to plan their spending, following the concept Plan – Pay – Purchase.

problem

Conceptualized and designed features that address the target audience’s needs, differentiate the brand from competitors, and make users feel that our application provides a better experience than simply scanning a QR code.

solution

Focused on vibrant and futuristic colors and design to appeal to the target audience, using shades of red, orange, green, blue, and purple. The design also emphasized key features such as setting financial goals, sending/receiving money, setting spending limits, currency conversion, deferred payments, and lending services.

result

Most users in the target audience appreciated the prototype, particularly for its bright and vibrant colors, modern and distinctive design compared to other similar platforms or applications, and the easy accessibility of various features.
