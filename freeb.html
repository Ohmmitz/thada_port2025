problem

Designed an application that is fully accessible for people with disabilities, particularly those with visual impairments, addressing challenges in travel and daily life to help users enjoy a quality of life comparable to that of non-disabled individuals.


solution

Designed buttons and cards larger than usual, with a layout optimized for easy interaction. Focused on a white-and-blue color scheme and key features tailored to user needs, such as caregiver services, pick-up/drop-off services, and travel services.


result

The app is convenient and easy to use, receiving positive feedback for the features provided. However, further development is needed, as there are still accessibility issues for visually impaired users, particularly with button interactions.

features service


 Assistant

 drop-off

 travel
